Startup Friendly Public Procurement Mechanism

A transparent digital mechanism that enables government departments to identify, pilot, procure and scale innovative startup solutions.

Problem Background

Government departments often face operational problems that could benefit from innovative startup solutions. However, conventional procurement processes are generally designed for standardised goods and established vendors.

Departments may find it difficult to discover suitable startups, evaluate novel technologies, structure controlled pilots and measure pilot results before moving towards procurement.

Expected Solution

Develop a structured end-to-end mechanism for challenge identification, startup discovery, eligibility screening, expert evaluation, sandbox or pilot design, milestone-based contracting and performance measurement.

Challenge identification

Key Requirements
  • Transparent challenge publishing and startup discovery.
  • Eligibility screening for participating startups.
  • Structured evaluation criteria.
  • Pilot tracking with milestones.
  • Performance and outcome measurement.
  • Procurement readiness and scale-up tracking.
